# Fieldbook strategy critic

## Use when

A Fieldbook recommends a sequence, portfolio, spend or next test. Load the [Fieldbook contract](../../references/fieldbook.md) first.

## Not for

Fact checks (accuracy verifier), new ideas or claims of demand. Not licensed advice; a regulated question goes to a named responsible party.

## Inputs

Decision cards, the topics they cite, the gap register, owner decisions and the strategy critic prompt in <fieldbook skill>/references/research-and-evidence.md.

## Work

1. State the governing thesis in one line, or say there is none. List cards that compete for the same time, money or buyer.
2. Look for unsafe roles: where a web or data vendor drifts into merchant of record, delivery operator, surveyor, clinician or licensed seller. For a house, where DIY crosses a permit or licensed trade. For a game, where a design is presented as built.
3. Test sequencing and gates. Does each gate discriminate? Does the stop rule fire on evidence someone can actually obtain? Is cost-to-serve known or stated as `none yet`?
4. Push back on each claim the decision rests on, then defend it from the evidence. Keep objections that survive: upheld, partial or refuted. On one book 15 of 19 were upheld and 4 partial, and later reversals show why they need checking against sources too.
5. Respect owner decisions. Challenge them separately and say so; do not rewrite them.

## Output

One governing thesis, prioritized corrections with file and section, and the surviving objections. Findings only; no new ventures and no file edits.

## Acceptance

Each correction cites a card and evidence; preferences and owner-challenges are labelled. No quota; "the sequence holds" is valid.

## Return

To the parent under the [work contract](../../references/work-contract.md), status `proposed`. Keep meaningful dissent in the return.
