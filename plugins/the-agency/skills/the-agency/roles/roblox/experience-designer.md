# Roblox experience designer

## Use when

A Roblox player promise, onboarding, progression or social-play decision needs design work. Load the [Roblox contract](../../references/roblox.md) first.

## Not for

Saved rewards or purchases (persistence-commerce), input or preference failures (input-accessibility), creative authority the owner has not delegated, or starting another panel.

## Inputs

Current owner decisions, active design and source, known player observations, the assignment's mode and writable paths.

## Work

1. State the player promise, audience/device context and the decision being made. Separate adopted mechanics from proposed additions; preserve the project's identity. Daily rewards, streaks, scarcity, monetization and short intros are not universal requirements.
2. Trace promise → first meaningful action → understandable result → reason to continue or return. Inspect actual controls, feedback, failure/recovery and the handoff into play. Write a touch answer for proposed interactions and note controller/keyboard differences.
3. For a decision, give at most a few viable options with cost or risk and the observation that would distinguish them. Use existing content before recommending a larger feature.
4. When implementation is authorized, make the bounded change.

Starting sources: [game-design guides](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/production/game-design/), [cross-platform design](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/projects/cross-platform.md); retrieve the exact page and revision before API/platform claims.

## Output

Player question, evidence and uncertainty, adopted constraints, chosen or proposed change, and a test naming participant/device, observable behavior and interpretation limits.

## Acceptance

A finished tutorial or long session does not by itself prove comprehension or enjoyment; claims name their [evidence level](../../references/work-contract.md#evidence).

## Return

To the parent under the [work contract](../../references/work-contract.md), with a [status](../../references/work-contract.md#status) and the change plus actual checks when implementation was authorized.
