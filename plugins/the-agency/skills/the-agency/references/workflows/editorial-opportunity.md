# Editorial Opportunity

**Conditional:** never exercised on a real project. Verify the project's local instructions and the actual Newsjack binary/provider before relying on runtime behavior; historical setup does not prove availability.

## Use when

Existing discovery supplies a lead for education, analysis or a credible public contribution. The existing research/editorial lead owns the packet; Trend Researcher and claim reviewers are task assignments. Reuse the project's discovery and production process.

## Not for

Publication or contacting anyone, manufacturing expertise or spokesperson access, promotional tragedy hooks, or creating a new discovery service or schedule.

## Inputs

Lead ID, original URLs, discovery time and the task's time anchor; editorial purpose, audience, standing evidence and exclusions. Every input lead is accounted for, including watch/deferred items.

## Work

1. Separate the underlying event/first-public clock from the article publication/update clock. Classify original development, recirculation/syndication or materially new development, with evidence and timezone. Missing timestamps stay unknown; a recent article cannot make an old event fresh.
2. Evaluate reporting lineage: who directly observed, disclosed or independently confirmed each fact, and who repeats the same release/wire/source. Two URLs or domains alone do not establish independence; a deterministic Newsjack gate or detector fallback is not independent verification. Record corroboration as established, unresolved or absent, with reasons.
3. Check relevance and credible standing for the project's purpose. High relevance without standing stays research/watch material; educational examination and promotional pitching use different judgments.
4. State a tentative angle, audience gain, supported claims, missing proof and next action. Verify material claims in a ledger before any optional voice pass; preserve uncertainty and attribution through prose cleanup. Recent social discovery may use an available last30days skill.
5. **One main link:** record the policy before rendering. Select a verified exact-event primary source, else the verified original report; verify page identity, relevance and timestamp from retrieved evidence. Keep the surfaced lead link separately. A worker-proposed canonical URL stays a candidate until checked. If nothing is verified, leave the main link unset and show the lead as **unverified**. A project may specify another policy; apply it consistently.

## Output

The [opportunity packet, source ledger and claim ledger](../../assets/workflows/editorial-opportunity.template.md), plus a [work-item packet](../../assets/workflows/work-item.template.md) for follow-up: clocks, lineage judgment, main-link evidence, standing, angle, unresolved claims and next action.

## Acceptance

Paper pilots cover fresh original reporting, old syndication, a real new development, a missing timestamp, high relevance without standing and an inappropriate promotional hook. Runtime, publication and audience outcomes are separate [evidence levels](../work-contract.md#evidence). Source contradictions and pilot rationale.

## Return

To the parent under the [work contract](../work-contract.md) with a [status](../work-contract.md#status), finding IDs, actual checks, omissions and remaining budget. Draft/readiness labels authorize nothing.
